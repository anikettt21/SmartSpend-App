# SpendSmart — Personal Expense Tracker

SpendSmart is a simple, beautiful, and fully offline personal expense tracker designed to help you take control of your finances. 

## 🌟 Why use SpendSmart?

- **Take Control of Your Money**: Easily record your daily expenses in just a few taps and understand where your money goes.
- **Stay on Budget**: Set a monthly budget goal and visually monitor your progress. The app will warn you when you're getting close to your limit!
- **Analyze Your Habits**: View clear, easy-to-understand category breakdowns to see exactly what you are spending the most on.
- **100% Offline & Private**: Your data never leaves your phone. SpendSmart works completely offline, ensuring your financial information remains strictly private and secure.
- **Clean & Modern Design**: Enjoy a distraction-free, easy-to-use interface built with Android's latest Material Design (including automatic dark mode!).

---

## 📱 Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Home Dashboard** | See your total spending for the current month, check your budget progress bar, and quickly view your 5 most recent transactions. |
| **Quick Add Expense** | Quickly add new expenses with the amount, category, date, and optional notes. |
| **All Expenses History** | View your full transaction history. Filter by month and easily navigate through your past spending. Long-press any expense to delete it if you made a mistake. |
| **Category Reports** | Get a clear visual breakdown of your spending by category for any given month to identify your top spending areas. |
| **Customizable Budget** | Go to the Settings screen to customize your monthly budget goal to fit your personal financial plans. |

## 📥 How to Install

You can install SpendSmart directly on your Android device using the provided APK file:

1. Go to the file list in this repository and download the `app-debug.apk` (or `SpendSmart.apk`) file.
2. Open the downloaded APK file on your Android phone.
3. Your phone may prompt you to **Allow installations from "Unknown Sources"**. Please enable this in your device settings to proceed. (This is a standard security prompt for apps installed outside the Google Play Store).
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to install the app.
5. Open SpendSmart and start tracking your expenses!

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

SpendSmart does not require an internet connection or any special permissions. All your expense data, budgets, and settings are stored locally on your own device. We do not collect, track, or share any of your personal or financial information.
